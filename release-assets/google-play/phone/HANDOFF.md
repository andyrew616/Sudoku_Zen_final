## Raw captures and treatment

`raw/` preserves eight untouched ADB PNG captures at 1440 × 3088: the six selected screens plus `00-home.png` and `07-resumed.png`. Associated foreground-checked UI XML is in `evidence/`. Only the package's own screens were retained.

A fresh puzzle was generated by the release app. Twelve correct values were entered through its actual keypad; no storage injection or test screen was used. The visible givens were read and the puzzle solved locally to choose correct inputs. The initial 35 filled digits were verified preserved after Save and return to menu → Resume Game. Manual notes were captured afterward, and the phone was left on the saved pause screen.

Treatment: crop OS status/navigation bars and unused background margins; resize uniformly with Lanczos; place the genuine rectangular screenshot on a restrained parchment-to-aqua backdrop; add a subtle outside shadow and a short headline using the app's Ubuntu typeface and ink/lavender/gold colours. No device frame, badges, claims about rankings, UI reconstruction, game-state image edits, exposure changes, or contrast changes were applied.

Do Not Disturb and Samsung Edge panels were temporarily adjusted for clean captures and restored to their original settings. Touch indicators and pointer overlays were already off. Audio/Auto Notes preferences were not changed.

## Quality review and evidence

A separate final review pass checked the contact sheet and raw/final screens for clear ordering, legible headlines, truthful feature claims, clean composition and absence of personal content or debug overlays in production assets. The first three show the difficulty choices, puzzle interaction and real help immediately. Raw menu/options images retain original OS status icons; these bars are completely outside the production crops.

`evidence/asset-validation.json` records file hashes, dimensions, mode and successful exact-pixel comparison of each composited app rectangle against its raw capture after the declared crop and uniform resize. `evidence/composition.json` documents every crop and placement. `evidence/build-verification.json` records provenance; `evidence/gameplay-inputs.json` records actual keypad entries. `compose.py` regenerates production files from the captures using Pillow and the repository-owned app font.

Current Google guidance checked: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en-GB . The chosen portrait format meets the recommended 1080 × 1920, 9:16 game screenshot format. Final acceptance remains subject to Andrew's manual listing review.

## Deliberate exclusions and follow-up

The home screen is preserved as a raw source but omitted from the curated set because difficulty selection communicates more in the first slot. Resume verification is evidence rather than a repeated gameplay marketing image. No Expert level, theme picker, stats, achievements or progression screen is implied: none was demonstrated by this release. Completion was excluded to avoid another gameplay image and keep the set focused.

Useful separate asset work: review the existing Play icon for consistency and prepare a 1024 × 500 feature graphic. Neither is included in this screenshot task.
