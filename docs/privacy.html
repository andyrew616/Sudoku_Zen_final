Zen Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

About this policy

LightGames develops Zen Sudoku for Android (package rew.lightgames.zensudoku).

This privacy policy explains how Zen Sudoku handles information when you use the app.

For privacy questions, contact zen.lightgames@gmail.com.

Puzzle progress and settings

Zen Sudoku stores puzzle progress, saved games and app settings locally on your device.

LightGames does not operate a server that receives or stores your puzzle progress, saved games, settings or gameplay history.

Zen Sudoku does not currently provide user accounts or login, cloud synchronisation, online leaderboards, in-app purchases, or subscriptions.

Advertising and consent

Zen Sudoku displays advertising using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

The app also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present and manage applicable advertising privacy and consent choices.

These Google services may communicate with Google and process information required to provide, measure and protect advertising services.

According to Google's documentation, information processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK can include:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Device and advertising identifiers

The exact information processed can depend on the SDK configuration, applicable privacy requirements and the choices available to you.

This information may be used for purposes including providing and measuring advertising, advertising analytics and fraud prevention.

Zen Sudoku does not currently integrate Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may nevertheless process its own diagnostic, interaction and measurement information.

Your advertising privacy choices

Where required, Zen Sudoku uses Google's consent tools to provide relevant privacy choices before advertising services are used.

Available advertising and privacy controls may also be managed through your Android device settings and Google's privacy controls.

Data retention and deletion

LightGames does not retain copies of your puzzle progress, saved games or app settings on its own servers.

You can remove Zen Sudoku's locally stored information by clearing the app's storage through Android settings. Doing so may permanently erase saved games, puzzle progress and app settings from that installation.

Android or Google may separately provide device backup and restore functionality depending on your device and settings. Any such platform-level backup is controlled by Android or Google rather than LightGames.

Information processed by Google through its advertising and consent services is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

If you contact LightGames by email, the information you choose to provide will be used to respond to your enquiry. Support correspondence may be retained where reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry or maintain appropriate support records. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the email address above, subject to any legal requirement to retain it.

Security

Puzzle progress and app settings are stored locally on your device rather than transmitted to LightGames servers.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted while being transmitted using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-party services

Zen Sudoku uses Google services for advertising and advertising consent. Google's own privacy policies govern Google's processing of information through those services.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google advertising information

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Zen Sudoku's features, third-party services or data-handling practices change.

The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
